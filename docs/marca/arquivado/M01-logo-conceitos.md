# M01 — Conceitos de logo

Hoje: ícone 3D de cronômetro prateado em degradê laranja-vermelho (gerado no Replit); ícones adaptativos do Android ainda são o padrão do Expo (chevron azul). Nome escrito de dois jeitos: "CronôBR" (app.json, Sobre) e "CronoBR" (cabeçalho, traduções, super PRD).

```
Create ONE brand exploration sheet, 1536x1024 landscape, plain #121212 background, for "CronoBR", an interval
workout timer app (Tabata, HIIT). Show FOUR numbered logo concepts side by side, each with: an app icon
(rounded square 1:1) on top, and below it the horizontal lockup icon + wordmark "CronoBR".
Palette only: orange #FF6B35, white #FFFFFF, near-black #121212, gray #2A2A2A / #B0B0B0. Flat vector, no 3D,
no metal, no gradients, no realistic stopwatch, must read well at 48 px.
1. "Anel": a thick orange ring with a gap at the top right like a progress arc, a short crown on top; icon on near-black.
2. "C de tempo": a bold letter C made of a progress arc, with a small play triangle inside; icon orange background, white mark.
3. "Intervalos": a stopwatch circle split into alternating orange and gray segments (work/rest), minimal crown.
4. "Pulso": a ring whose bottom becomes a small heartbeat/zigzag line, crown on top; icon on near-black.
Wordmark in every lockup: bold geometric sans, "Crono" white, "BR" orange, spelled exactly "CronoBR" (no accent).
Under each concept a tiny gray caption with its number and name. No other text.
```
