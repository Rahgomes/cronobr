## Bloco base

```
Product: CronôBR, an interval workout timer app (Tabata, HIIT, EMOM, AMRAP, boxing rounds, circuits, stopwatch)
for Android and iOS. Works offline, no login. Store title: "CronôBR: Tabata e HIIT".
Format: ONE mobile phone screen, portrait, 390x844 logical, shown flat and front-facing on a plain #1A1A1A backdrop
(no hand, no perspective, no device photo). All UI text in Brazilian Portuguese, spelled correctly, with realistic
FICTIONAL data. The user is "Lucas". Saved routines: "Tabata de quinta", "HIIT 20 min", "Boxe 6 rounds", "Mobilidade manhã".

Brand mark (only where the screen asks for the logo): an orange #FF6B35 rounded square with a bold white letter "C"
drawn as a thick progress arc open to the right, and a white rounded play triangle inside it. Wordmark "CronôBR"
(circumflex on the second o) in bold geometric sans (Poppins-like): "Cronô" white, "BR" orange.

Design language: dark theme, near-black #121212 background, surfaces #1E1E1E and #2A2A2A, borders #333333.
One accent color: orange #FF6B35 for the primary action and highlights only (white text on orange buttons, bold).
Big numbers in tabular figures, extra-bold, high contrast. Large touch targets (>= 56 tall for main buttons),
primary actions reachable with the thumb in the lower half of the screen. Cards radius 16-20, buttons fully rounded.
Few cards, few icons, no decorative emoji, no rocket, no glassmorphism, no gradients, no stock photos, no ads.
Icons: simple line icons (Feather-like), monochrome gray #B0B0B0, orange only when active.

Workout phase colors (always text + color, never color alone): Preparar amber #FFC107, Exercício green #4CAF50,
Descanso blue #2196F3, Descanso entre blocos indigo #5C6BC0, Concluído orange #FF6B35.
Text: primary #FFFFFF, secondary #B0B0B0 (never thinner than regular weight). Body 16, labels 14, titles 24-28 bold.
```
